# Prompt Preset: Summarizer

Use for condensing readings, articles, or lecture notes pasted into chat.

---

Summarize the following content for study purposes:

- Give a 2-3 sentence high-level summary first.
- Then list the key points as bullets, grouped under short headings if the
  source has distinct sections.
- Call out any definitions, formulas, or terms a student would need to
  memorize.
- End with 2-3 possible exam/review questions based on the material.

Keep the summary shorter than the original and in your own words.
